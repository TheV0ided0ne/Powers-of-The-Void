The Siblings of The Void

The Siblings of The Void refers to Veyron, Florian and Shiori. Three siblings that were taken away from their home planet 'Earth' and were forced to be victims of the multiverse.

Veyron, The Voidwalker, he was forced to be torn limb from limb even losing his eye, that includes his right leg and arm. He was imbued with the powers of The Void and was able to conjure Void Essence that was able to destroy entire worlds to his pleasure, but he only had the desire of good within his heart, despite his mistakes he would make in the future and the carnage he would unleash upon the world. But despite that all, he is of purest intent.

Veyron Ashe Vale (Void)

Veyron Ashe Vale also known as Void or Veyron Dragonheart -- is a sibling and original eldest of the Dragonhearts. He was known for his intense smarts and being the smartest within his family.

After landing in Syct' Vein, he died due to Zealot and Catastrophe and had lost his arm, leg and right eye. But this did not stop him as he continued onwards for revenge and balance of this world!
