Veyron Ashe Vale also known as Void or Veyron Dragonheart - is a sibling and original eldest of the Dragonhearts. He was known for his intense smarts and being the smartest within his family.

After landing in Syct' Vein, he died due to Zealot and Catastrophe and had lost his arm, leg and right eye. But this did not stop him as he continued onwards for revenge and balance of this world!

Resources

• Void Essence

Void Essence is a resource that can be regenerated by consuming blocks infused with The Void, such as amethyst and obsidian. You can also be nearby these blocks and regenerate too!

• Blood

Blood is a resource that can be regenerated by killing enemies, even if they have no blood... for some reason- But despite that obvious logic flaw, you can use blood to pull yourself into a frenzy and use some abilities!

• Decay

Decay is a negative resource that grows overtime by using abilities. You can only lower this decay by not using abilities for a certain time period, otherwise you slowly decay and begin to die. The only exception is using Vampiric Rage and killing enemies to quench your decays thirst.

Passives

• Darkness Incarnate - Positive

Whilst within the darkness, you begin to ease your decaying body and start to adapt to it, gaining night vision, becoming faster with speed and haste, and also healing upon kills whilst inside of this state.

• Fire Within - Positive

Due to your immense healing factor and Voidwalker body, you can withstand any basic fire and can withstand lava for a short time, eventually taking damage from lava after 10 seconds.

• Forced Regeneration - Neutral

Consume Void Essence over time to heal yourself, you cannot toggle this and this could be bad for you in some situations but overall is quite useful when you have a plentiful resource!

• Undead - Neutral

Due to your undead nature of being part vampiric, you have all the debuffs of an undead mob except for burning in the daylight but also all the positives. You take more damage from Smite enchantments but can breathe underwater and cannot suffocate.

• Heavy Armor - Negative

Despite you being undead and able to breathe underwater, you cannot move as efficiently in the water and rain due to your heavy armor, granting you slowness whenever it is raining or you are submerged in the water.

• Vampiric Laws - Negative

Due to you being part vampire, some vampiric laws apply to you. You cannot touch silver as it decays you IMMENSELY and causes you extreme harm, only being countered by having the resistance effect.

Abilities

• Rift Slash - Level 1 Unlock

Slash a rift within the Void with your extremely sharp void claws or instantly teleport yourself. You can either charge this ability and teleport over 200 blocks away, or teleport over 30 blocks instantly.
